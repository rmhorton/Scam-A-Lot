# Scam-A-Lot

**Political satire in songs, radio, theater, and protest performance.**

In the kingdom of **Scam-A-Lot**, reality itself has become media spectacle. The state-media system sells outrage, certainty, nationalism, masculinity branding, billionaire worship, endless war, propaganda, and emotional simplification.

**There is resistance.**

## What Is Scam-A-Lot?

Scam-A-Lot is **not one fixed musical**.

It is a reusable repertoire of political satire designed to work in many settings:

- theatrical productions
- cabaret
- protests and demonstrations
- radio and podcasts
- online video
- concerts and political events
- new productions assembled from existing material

A performer might use one song.

A theater company might combine several songs with sketches and commercials.

A protest group might adapt a chorus for a demonstration.

A radio producer might build an entirely different program from the same library.

The pieces are meant to travel.

## Radio Free Scam-A-Lot

**Radio Free Scam-A-Lot** is the first full-length production created from the repertoire.

It takes place inside the state-media system of the fictional kingdom of Scam-A-Lot, where broadcasters sell certainty, outrage, patriotism, masculinity, billionaire worship, and endless war.

Then a pirate radio station begins breaking into the signal.

At the center of the story are:

- **Dave**, an insecure state-radio DJ who slowly begins to question the system he serves.
- **Flower**, a pirate broadcaster whose resistance evolves from ideological certainty toward empathy and human connection.

The show mixes songs, talk-radio segments, advertisements, propaganda announcements, news, and pirate interruptions.

## Make Something With It

The material is meant to be **performed, adapted, broadcast, rearranged, and shared**.

If you are a singer, musician, actor, theater company, cabaret performer, protest organizer, radio producer, filmmaker, podcaster, or political satirist, you are invited to explore the repertoire and find pieces that work for you.

- adaptations and arrangements
- broadcasts
- recordings and videos
- attribution
- licensing

If you use the material, please let us know where it goes.

## Songs, Songbooks, and Future Productions

The project continues to grow beyond *Radio Free Scam-A-Lot*.

Individual songs are being developed so they can stand on their own in theater, protest, cabaret, radio, and other settings.

Some will also be organized into thematic **songbooks** and future productions.

The goal is not to build one definitive musical.

The goal is to build a repertoire.

---

## How Scam-A-Lot Is Made

Scam-A-Lot uses AI-assisted tools extensively for brainstorming, research, lyric development, musical prototyping, voice production, visual design, and software development.

The work is shaped through an iterative human process of selection, writing, revision, direction, editing, and assembly.

The project also explores how inexpensive modern production tools can help independent political art move quickly from an idea to something that can actually be heard, performed, and reused.
