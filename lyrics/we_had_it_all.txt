# WE HAD IT ALL

Fast 140 BPM train-beat country-rock. Gruff male baritone, rapid clear storytelling, acoustic rhythm guitar, twangy electric guitar, upright bass, and driving snare. Short punchy refrains, shouted political bridge, immediate vocal. Compact radio-single arrangement with no solos, breaks, extra repetitions, or fade.

[VERSE 1]

Old Charlie at the hardware store
Knew every bolt and screw;
He’d listen to your problem
And tell you what to do.

I hated when they closed it—
It was like my second home.
But this wrench was seven dollars less,
So I bought it on my phone.

[CHORUS]

We had it all, we had it made,
High wages, prices low;
Now Main Street’s boarded and decayed—
We had it all, you know.

[VERSE 2]

We bought this house on one income;
Its value rose tenfold.
That rising equity served us well—
Our house was good as gold.

Our kids say they can’t buy here;
The times are just too hard.
But don’t build apartments down the street—
Not in my backyard.


[VERSE 3]

The owners shipped our union jobs
Where labor cost them less;
We bought the cheaper goods they sent
And called it a success.

Now bring those jobs and smokestacks back,
But keep our river clean;
And keep the regulations out—
Well, you know what we mean.


[CHORUS]

Life was good, we had it made;
Bring back the days that we recall,
When we did great with foreign trade—
We really had it all.

[VERSE 4]

Public funds built dams and bridges;
Federal loans lit rural homes.
But we built this town ourselves—
We did it on our own.

Those public works still serve us,
Though the bridges groan and crack;
We want to cut our taxes now
To bring those good times back.

[BRIDGE]

But we don’t want excuses—
We know who caused the fall:

Bureaucrats!
Globalists!
Radical-left lunatics!

They came and took it all!

[FINAL CHORUS]

We had it all, we had it made,
Wages high and prices low;
Our kids live in our basements now—
Where did that good life go?

They stole our children's future
And left a vacant shopping mall;
They put our nation on the skids—
But once we had it all.
