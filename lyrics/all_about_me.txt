All About Me

Satirical Broadway patter operetta with smug bass-baritone lead vocals, dry aristocratic delivery, patriotic brass accents, piano, and light marching snare. Gilbert & Sullivan-inspired political satire. Dense lyrics, rapid vocal delivery, continuous singing, and constant forward momentum. Minimal instrumental interludes, minimal introductions, minimal transitions between sections, and no extended solos. Through-composed structure with new lyrics continuously advancing the story. Avoid repeating chorus lines, avoid repeating chant sections, avoid musical reprises, avoid extended outros, and avoid instrumental padding between verses. Compact theatrical arrangement focused on delivering the lyrics as efficiently as possible. Cheerful crowd responses gradually become unsettling propaganda slogans. No instrumental breaks. Vocals nearly continuous.



[KING]

They all wanted me
To talk a bit about my history
About how I came to be
Of course I would agree,
It's all about me!
And everybody loves to see
My deep humility!

[CORRUPTION]

[KING]

Father taught me business
When I was very small
“The law is just a framework, son —
Connections conquer all!”

Inspectors came inspecting
But our friends came through
And every politician learned
What gratitude can do!

And thus I learned in childhood
What distinguishes the great:
The rules are merely obstacles
That winners renegotiate!

[CROWD RESPONSE]

Charge Them More! Raise the Price!
Expensive means it must be nice!
If they pay too much, you must be smart!
The higher the price, the greater the art!


[SCOREKEEPING]

[KING]

The country club became my school
Where mighty men convene
To celebrate achievement
And admire expensive green!

A gentleman need never cheat
If his people keep the score
And those who count too carefully
Don't matter anymore!

The caddies learned discretion
No awkward questions reached my ear!
My golf balls wander off somewhere
Then as if by magic reappear!

[CROWD RESPONSE]

Charge Them More! Raise the Price!
Expensive means it must be nice!
Keeping score is the secret of success!
Losers play by rules, winners just apply finesse!

[SPINNING FAILURE]

[KING]

Some men are crushed by failure
Their confidence undone
But every grand catastrophe
Can be a triumph if it’s spun!

My casinos folded gloriously!
My airline disappeared!
My steaks became collectible!
My shareholders got sheared!

My university folded
My pizza ads were bland
But every bold disaster simply
Amplified the brand!

[CROWD RESPONSE]

Charge Them More! Raise the Price!
Expensive means it must be nice!
Failure isn't real 'til you admit it!
Spin it right and they'll forget it!


[POLITICS]

[KING]

Politics confirmed at last
The lessons of my youth:
The people do not care for facts —
They believe what feels like truth!

They need a mighty champion
To face their enemy
And every accusation proved
How dangerous I must be!

For shame is just a leash they use
To keep the weak in line
And once you transcend consequence
The rules do not apply!

[CROWD RESPONSE]

For men immune to scandal
