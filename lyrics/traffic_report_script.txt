# Traffic Report

JIM:
It's 8:17. Time for your morning Traffic Report. We go now to Chip Chopper in Traffic One, live over Palm Beach. Chip?

[Helicopter rotors fade in.]
CHIP:
THANKS, JIM! From up here you can really appreciate the heavy traffic this morning around the Mar-a-Lago interchange. The usual morning pickups are running a few minutes behind schedule, but movement toward the mansion is otherwise smooth. Recruiters report brief delays picking up teenage girls from the spa district.

CHIP:
Looking southbound, we're seeing a steady stream of referrals arriving from local high schools and shopping malls, so expect heavier volume through the late-morning rush.

CHIP:
Officials describe conditions as completely routine and say they have no plans to interfere with the normal flow of traffic.
[SFX: Helicopter fades out.]

JIM:
How are things looking at the airport?

[Helicopter rotors fade in.]
CHIP:
A little congestion at the private terminal, Jim. Several flights are preparing for departure to Little Saint James, but ground crews expect things to clear once the morning rush is airborne. If you're headed that way, allow a few extra minutes.

CHIP:
Back to you.
[SFX: Helicopter fades out.]

JIM:
Thanks, Chip. That's your Traffic Report... keeping commerce moving.
