Reflections

Theatrical orchestral storytelling. Elderly male baritone, nostalgic, pompous, bombastic. Brisk pacing, continuous vocals, minimal instrumental breaks. Rich piano, strings and brass. Bridge becomes rapid spoken-sung patter, then returns to dignified reflection. Prioritize lyrical clarity over instrumentation.

[Verse 1: Nostalgic retrospective]

There comes a time in every life
When one reflects on what he’s done
With towers reaching toward the heavens
What admiration I have won!

[CHOIR]

Reflections, reflections
How beautiful they seem
Reality can be so harsh
But reflections are a dream

[Verse 2: Demonstration of skill]

I shall build some thing enduring
Worthy of a head of state
Among the nation’s monuments
I shall leave my mark in REAL estate!

And so I chose a worthy project
Majestic, elegant, and cool
A monument to my skill and vision
A glimmering REFLECTING POOL!

[CHOIR]

Reflections, reflections
A mirror cannot lie
The greatness of the kingdom
Is reflected in my eye

[Verse 3: Triumph]

Graphs displayed the scale and grandeur
Statistics left the crowd aghast
Longer than the tall skyscrapers!
The fame of my accomplishments will last!

I hired friends I knew could do it
No need to waste time taking bids
I’m the unitary executive—
Who cares what the law forbids?

I chose a liner, extra tough
My favorite shade of blue
A knife can't even cut this stuff!
This is what great builders do

We filled the pool with river water
The ducks and geese made quite a show
My dark blue lining warmed the pool and
Encouraged everything to grow!

Admire the majestic beauty!
Don't think about the war.
Don't think about the high gas prices
Or what you're paying at the store!

[BRIDGE - FRANTIC]

Algae grew after all that construction.
But we'll clean it up with suction!

No sooner were improvements made
Than they took some form of knife or blade
They snuck right past security
And cut it very violently!

Antifa built an algae farm!
Traitors always plotting harm!
Fake news! Vandals! Sabotage!
They hid my pool in camouflage!

[Verse 4: Not important]

(ANNOYED)

That patriotic symbols mean

Don't pester me about this pool!
We know exactly what the source is!
I have an empire to rule!
So now I'll build some MORE GOLF COURSES!
