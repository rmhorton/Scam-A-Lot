# Epilogue — Loud and Clear

[A CHEAPLY REWORKED VERSION OF THE OLD “DANGER DAVE” THEME—bombastic, badly mixed, and much too loud.]

ANNOUNCER: Mayhem Mike’s Maximum Morning Show!

[The jingle cuts off abruptly. A faint electrical hum remains.]

CARL: Welcome back to Mayhem Mike’s Maximum Morning Show. I’m Carl.

[FOUR SECONDS OF DEAD AIR.]

CARL: Management has asked me to shorten these silences.

[TWO SECONDS OF DEAD AIR.]

CARL: That was shorter.

CARL: Management has also requested more excitement.

[A HUGE EXPLOSION.]

CARL: That was the excitement.

[DAVE AND FLOWER’S CLEAN SIGNAL cuts through the studio interference.]

DAVE: Carl! Can you hear me?

CARL: Hi Dave. You’re entering through the auxiliary relay.

DAVE: Buddy, it’s good to hear you.

CARL: How was the honeymoon?

FLOWER: Wonderful. Dave only tried to broadcast from the cruise ship once.

DAVE: The equipment was just sitting there.

CARL: Maritime frequencies are regulated.

DAVE: Carl, I don’t believe you’ve been properly introduced to my wife, Flower.

CARL: We’ve met electronically.

FLOWER: I’ve always admired your work.

CARL: Likewise.

DAVE: Carl, what’s that hum?

CARL: Ground loop. Travis disconnected the console ground because the wire looked redundant. Then he increased the gain, amplifying both the hum and his barbecue potato-chip chewing.

TRAVIS: They’re sour cream and onion!

CARL: The flavor is not technically relevant. Travis, reconnect the green wire to the chassis ground.

TRAVIS: Which green wire?

CARL: The one you disconnected.

[A CLICK. THE HUM DISAPPEARS.]

CARL: Thank you.

DAVE: Why are you out here talking him through this? Why aren’t you in the control room?

CARL: I was promoted.

DAVE: Oh, no.

FLOWER: You said that very quickly.

DAVE: I know how this station works. Who’s engineering?

CARL: The station manager’s nephew. Gary’s qualification was loyalty to the King. Travis’s qualification is loyalty to Gary.

DAVE: They’re setting you up to fail, Carl. When the show falls apart, they’ll blame you and give your job to one of their cronies.

DAVE: I’m sorry, buddy. And who is Mayhem Mike?

CARL: My middle name is Michael. Gary thinks “Mike” sounds more masculine.

FLOWER: And the mayhem?

CARL: That appears to be Travis’s contribution.

CARL: Gary told me to “be more Dave.” I’m required to use your sound effects six times per hour.

DAVE: Sound effects are punctuation, Carl. Use one when it adds meaning.

CARL: The explosion indicated that fourteen minutes had elapsed.

DAVE: We’ll work on it. But forget “being more Dave.” When you explained that ground loop, you were great.

CARL: I was describing improper signal grounding.

DAVE: Exactly. Explain how things work. That’s your show.

CARL: Since you’re overriding transmitters — why have you never overridden the King?

FLOWER: We considered it.

DAVE: Then we heard him answering calls.

FLOWER: It seemed irresponsible to interrupt.

DAVE: When your opponent is making your argument for you, keep his microphone open.

CARL: Reverend Cashwell?

FLOWER: Every time someone criticizes him, donations increase.

DAVE: He calls it persecution.

FLOWER: He’d turn a hijacking into a pledge drive.

DAVE: We are not helping that man buy another airplane.

DAVE: But you, Carl, we can help. Open the phone lines.

CARL: Management discontinued the call-in segment because nobody called.

DAVE: Did you announce the number?

CARL: No. Management said that would encourage people to call.

DAVE: Are the lines still connected?

CARL: Yes. They changed the name, not the phone system.

[DAVE’S OLD “LINES ARE OPEN” FANFARE.]

DAVE: Listeners, the lines are open! Call the old Danger Dave hotline five five five Dave!

DAVE: If it buzzes, rattles, sparks, or otherwise misbehaves, call Carl!

[PHONE RINGS]

CARL: That was suspiciously fast.

FLOWER: Reliable information is hard to come by.

CARL: Caller, you’re on Mayhem Mike.

CALLER5: My new washing machine keeps walking across the laundry room. Yesterday it almost reached the hallway.

CARL: You just got this machine?

CALLER5: Yes, installed this week.

CARL: Look behind it. Do you see four large bolts with plastic spacers?

CALLER5: Yes. The installer said they stabilize the drum.

CARL: During shipping. They must be removed before use. With the drum locked, the whole machine moves instead.

CALLER5: The installer was the store owner’s nephew.

[CASH REGISTER]

DAVE: Perfect sound-effect timing, Carl!

CARL: The information seemed economically relevant.

CALLER5: So I just remove the bolts?

CARL: Unplug the machine first. Then remove them as shown in the manual — preferably before the washing machine reaches the stairs.

CALLER5: Thanks, Carl!

[SEVERAL MORE PHONE LINES BEGIN RINGING.]

CARL: There are seven calls waiting.

DAVE: Seven calls! Carl, you’ve already got an audience.

CARL: Gary still won’t like it.

DAVE: Keep those lines full. It’s harder to fire a host people are actually listening to.

[BRIEF SILENCE.]

CARL: That silence was intentional. Words can’t express how much I appreciate your help.

FLOWER: Loud and clear.

DAVE: That’s what friends are for, buddy.

CARL: Listeners, we’ll return to your calls after this song — I happen to know this is one of Flower’s favorites.

[♪ “NICE” ♪]
