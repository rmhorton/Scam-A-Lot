## Scene 5: Wealth proves incorruptibility.

ANNOUNCER:
Welcome back to The Royal Hour.

KING:
People ask me all the time, "Your Majesty, aren't you worried that wealthy people might try to influence you?"
 ...
Ridiculous.
 ...
I'm one of the richest kings in history. Nobody could possibly buy me. I'm already too rich for that to work.
 ...
So someone gives me a gift... maybe a beautiful airplane. ... They're not buying influence. They're showing respect. And frankly, they should. They'd better. I'm a very powerful king, believe me.
 ...
If they invest in one of my companies, they are not influencing me; it just proves they recognize how influential I am.
 ...
You have to worry about bribes with poor politicians. They get greedy, they start selling influence. Rich politicians are too rich to be greedy. Everybody knows that. Bribing rich people is a stupid idea. It costs too much. It's not a good investment.

KING:
People ask me all the time, "Your Majesty, where do your ideas come from?" Some people think ideas come from thinking. ... Wrong.  ... Ideas come from leadership.
 ... 
Sometimes somebody says something that sounds like an idea. It gives ME an idea, and I'll say, "I have this tremendous idea." Then everybody agrees it's a tremendous idea. That's leadership.

KING:
You know who else had ideas? Leonardo da Vinci. Very talented fellow. Not as good a businessman as me, but a talented guy, from what I understand. Never licensed his name. Big mistake. Every painting should have had "Leonardo" right across the bottom. He left a lot of money on the table. Nobody talks about that. Or helicopters.
 ... 
Some people say I change the subject. I don't change the subject. It's called the weave. Very sophisticated. You have to have a very high IQ. Very few people can do it.

KING:
I've always had a very good vocabulary. The best words. ... My favorite word? "Tariff"! Such a beautiful word - maybe the most beautiful word in the English language. People don't appreciate beautiful words because—
[Fade out in the middle of the last sentence]
