ARE WE GREAT YET?

Satirical Broadway propaganda anthem with theatrical call-and-response between a nervous female soloist and a loud patriotic male crowd chorus. Starts small and triumphant, then grows progressively bigger, louder, and more unstable with brass fanfares, military drums, chanting crowds, and sweeping orchestration. Grand imperial spectacle, dark humor, authoritarian energy, and increasingly desperate patriotic slogans. A single female narrator sings every question and every verse throughout the song. Male voices only appear in the propaganda responses and crowd chants.

===

(female narrator)

The heralds say we’re winning bigly.
The trumpets never seem to rest. 
Everybody says we’ve we're always winning,
But...  are we great yet?

(male crowd)

We’ve made the kingdom Great Again!
Greater than it’s ever been!
Nobody’s ever seen such greatness!
Everybody says we win!

[PROSPERITY]

(female narrator)

I mean no disrespect, my lords, 
But bread grows dear and hard to get. 
I’m patriotic as can be, 
but are we great yet?

(male crowd)

We are Great! Tremendously!
Historic levels of prosperity!
Patriots pay proudly, peasant!
You know that Freedom isn't Free!

[POLITICAL AND MILITARY LEADERSHIP]

(female narrator)

The northern kingdom shut its gates… 
Our western neighbors seem upset. 
Our oldest allies drift away, 
So ...  are we great yet?

(male crowd)

Nobody leads the world like us!
Dependence made them weak and soft!
If allies now defend themselves
That proves our leadership paid off!

[TECHNOLOGICAL LEADERSHIP]

Scholars are fleeing across the seas. 
Inventors find their needs unmet. 
While eastern empires build new wonders
So are we great yet?

We are great and getting greater!
We're going to the moon and maybe mars!
We are great! Off the charts!
Soon we will all have self-driving cars!

(female narrator)

We once spoke of equal justice

Too much freedom is a weakness!
