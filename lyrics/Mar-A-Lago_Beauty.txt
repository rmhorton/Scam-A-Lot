Mar-A-Lago Beauty

Brassy Broadway comedy with 1950s beauty-pageant glamour. Confident female lead, cheerful sincerity, crisp diction, witty patter. Light swing, 130 BPM. Piano, walking bass, punchy brass, female backing harmonies. Catchy glamorous chorus; upbeat even when the lyrics turn dark.
