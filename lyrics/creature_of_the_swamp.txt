CREATURE OF THE SWAMP

Brisk late-1950s sci-fi / horror-comedy novelty rock ’n’ roll movie theme. Cheerfully catchy music tells an increasingly sinister monster story. Animated male lead, dramatic trailer announcer, and small male group shouting “Creature of the Swamp!” Twanging guitar, pounding piano, upright bass, punchy drums, honking sax, theremin shrieks, and orchestral stings. Clear lyrics, energetic call-and-response. Build from jaunty origin story to chaotic feeding frenzy and a big final revelation. Compact arrangement, continuous vocals, minimal instrumental breaks.


[OMINOUS FANFARE WITH ORCHESTRA AND THEREMIN]

[THEREMIN SHRIEK]

[VERSE 1 — ORIGIN]

From the fetid banks
Of the Potomac River,
Comes a dreadful creature
That will make you shiver!

He was born without a conscience,
But with wealth and power—
A sociopathic narcissist
Growing vainer by the hour.

He put his name on buildings,
He learned his act in showbiz;
He never learned to compromise—
What’s yours is his!


[SHORT REFRAIN — GROUP]

Creature of the Swamp!
See him slither, hear him stomp!
He sold you fear and promised change—
Now the whole thing bears his name!

[VERSE 2 — CAMPAIGN]

He came to Washington shouting,
“I’ll drain it dry!
I alone can save you—
Stand back and stand by!”

The oil men liked his promises,
The bankers liked his roar;
The preachers blessed his appetite—
The crowds demanded more.

[VERSE 3 — THE BARGAIN]

He will flatter and cajole.
He will threaten and berate.
He calls it making deals—
But he does not negotiate.

He gobbled up the lobbyists
One by one—
But they teamed up with the creature
And joined in the fun.

The donors brought him tribute
The sycophants all bowed;
And the creature kept growing
As he worked the crowd.

A room at his hotel!
A round at his course!
A shiny new tribute
From a shadowy source!

Whatever your hustle,
Whatever your scheme,
Just toss him some treasure
And tell him he’s supreme!

[CHORUS]

Creature of the Swamp!
Watch him grow, hear him stomp!
Gold in his claws and green in his veins,
Feeding on favors and government gains!

Creature of the Swamp!
Watch him wallow, watch him romp!
He didn’t drain it, as he’d claimed—
He fed! He grew! And he became
the—

Creature of the Swamp!

[BRIDGE — BRISK]

The psychologists warned us:
“Don’t let it feed!”
But nothing can satisfy
Such bottomless greed.

It gobbled up power,
It trampled the laws,
And tore the Constitution
Apart with its claws.

[VERSE 4 — FEEDING FRENZY]

He put his name on the dollar,
Put his name on the Center;
The people stayed home
And the artists wouldn’t enter.

His Justice Department
Sued his IRS
For ten billion dollars—

[SPOKEN — CREATURE]
“But I might settle for less!”

[FINAL CHORUS]

Creature of the Swamp!
Hear him slither, hear him stomp!
Cash in his teeth and a crown on his head,
Growing more hungry the more he is fed!

He promised the people he’d drain it dry—
But the national debt kept climbing sky-high!

The swamp grew bigger,
Filled up to the brim;
The creature got richer—
And the creature was HIM!

[THEREMIN SHRIEK]

[SPOKEN]

He came to clean up Washington...
Now the country will never feel clean again!

Creature of the Swamp!

Creature of the Swamp!
