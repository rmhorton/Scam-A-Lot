Enthralled
(theme song from the movie)

Epic end-credits theme from a gothic vampire film. Mythic cinematic orchestral folk ballad in a minor key with haunting female vocals, piano, strings, French horns, choir, and subtle percussion building steadily to a powerful finale. Beautiful, mournful, ancient, and tragic rather than frightening. Continuous vocal with no spoken dialogue, no instrumental breaks, and a long orchestral sustain after the final line.

[Verse 1: Weariness / The Invitation]

When watchmen tire of keeping watch
And sentries cease to roam,
The oldest shadows in the world
Come searching for your home.

They never batter down the gate
Or strike the lock apart.
They wait until the welcome comes
From some exhausted heart.

He thanked us for our courtesy,
So gracious and so kind!
In hindsight we can see the cost;
It seemed small at the time.


[Verse 2: Denial (self-deception) / The Reflection]

The mirror made no accusations,
It showed us what was true.
The monster never feared the glass—
He feared what we might view.

So first he taught us not to look.
Then taught us who to trust.
At last we called the mirror false,
And crushed it into dust.

We said the looking glass was biased,
Corrupted by design.
Far easier to doubt the glass
Than question our own minds.


[Verse 3: Devotion / The Bargain]

He knew each hunger of the heart,
Each wound we'd never named.
He never asked us what was true—
Only who we blamed.

A promise here, a favor there,
A whisper in the night.
We scarcely noticed, one by one,
Our wrong became our right.

We learned that truth was troublesome,
But loyalty was clear.
We fed the master faithfully;
He taught us who to fear

[Verse 4: Enthrallment / Open the gates of the castle]

We opened wide the castle gate
And laid our weapons by.
We did not make the country great
We let the wolves inside.

In every street and every home
They prowl and snarl and bite
No law restrains where they may roam.
His children of the night.

And as his sharp fangs pierce your throat,
Your eyes will open wide.
Be more careful how you vote —
And who you let inside.
