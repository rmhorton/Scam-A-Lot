# Heads We Win

Fast militaristic patter march with clipped, commanding male baritone vocals and a forceful rally chorus. Rapid clear diction, minimal intro, continuous vocals, no instrumental solos. Stern, aggressive and propagandistic.

[VERSE 1]

Immigrants came by the millions,
And liberals let them stay;
They mooch off all the benefits
That good taxpayers pay.

And when election season comes,
We know which way they’ll side:
Demand their papers at the door
Before they vote inside.

[CHORUS]

We know they cheated — if we get beat.
If we win, they tried but failed.
If we find cheating, we'll bring receipts.
If we don’t, they hid the trail.

Heads we win, tails you cheat!

[VERSE 2]

The cheating started long ago,
Before our first campaign:
The Apprentice lost the Emmy
Through politics and games.

When Obama beat Mitt Romney
It was a "total sham!"
We would've won that vote quite easily,
Like we’d win Vietnam.

In twenty sixteen Cruz stole Iowa;
Colorado shut us out;
Hillary got more votes than us,
So throw those numbers out!

[VERSE 3]

Mail ballots go to registered homes
They check each signature on file;
If no poll watcher sees your face—
You can cheat by a mile!

So vote where we can watch you,
And count them the same day;
Late ballots are just made-up votes
To take our lead away.


[VERSE 4]

We searched the rolls and found no fraud—
That proves the fraud runs deep;
The people meant to look for it
Were paid to stay asleep!

And if their audit says "all clear,"
They’re partners in the crime;
We’ll audit every auditor
And lock them up this time!


[CHORUS]
Heads we win, tails you cheat!


[VERSE 5]

Voting machines have secret switches
Controlled across the sea;
They turn one honest vote for us
To seven for the enemy.

No one understands the code—
That’s how we know it lies;
Use watermarked paper, counted twice
By patriots we recognize.

[BRIDGE]

We’ll build a list in Washington
Of every citizen;
With only days to finish it
Before the votes begin.

We’ll barcode every envelope,
Then launch it untested, at scale;
If Washington can’t find your name,
We’ll stop it in the mail!

[VERSE 6]

We won a massive landslide
In twenty-twenty-four;
The cheaters somehow lost their touch—
But now they’re back for more.

Our approval rating's dropping;
We'll act without delay!
The ballots mail next Tuesday—
So change the rules today!


[CHORUS]

If we win, they tried but failed.

If we don’t, they hid the trail.

Heads we win, tails you cheat!
