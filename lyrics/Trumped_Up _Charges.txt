Trumped Up Charges

Upbeat 1960s pop-country with bright acoustic guitar, light drums, walking bass, and warm vocal harmonies. Cheerful, catchy, almost nostalgic, while the lyrics grow progressively darker. Minimal intro, continuous vocals, no instrumental breaks, ending with a confident sing-along chorus.

[Verse 1: Justice as retribution.]

Lady Justice used to wear
A blindfold on her face.
She was not supposed to recognize
Your party or your race.

The law was built to limit kings,
To bind the mighty, too.
Once it serves the whims of rulers,
It stops protecting you.

Trumped up charges!
Someone always gets the blame!
Yesterday they cried "Witch hunt!"
Today they light the flame.

Trumped up charges!
Friends get open doors
Justice isn't law or fairness,
Justice settles scores.

Another thing to fabricate.

Trumped-up charges!
Someone has to take the fall.

Trumped-up charges!
Verdict first, then evidence.
Justice isn't finding facts—

Loyalty has privileges.

Trumped-up charges!
