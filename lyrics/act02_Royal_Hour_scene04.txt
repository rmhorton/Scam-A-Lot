## Scene 4 — Confidence proves correctness

ANNOUNCER:
We now return to The Royal Hour.

CALLER5:
Good morning, Your Majesty. I have a question uh—

KING: 
Exactly! Nobody gets better questions than I do. The quality is incredible. Some radio hosts get ordinary questions. I get tremendous questions. The smartest questions. My listeners ask questions nobody else would even think to ask. That's why this is the number-one radio program in the kingdom. People tell me callers spend weeks preparing them. Thank you for bringing that up.

ANNOUNCER:
We have another caller on the line, your highness.

CALLER6 [Nervously]:
Uh, good morning, Your Majesty. Scientists are warning that —

KING:
Scientists! Always studying! If they already knew everything, they wouldn't have to keep studying, would they? Think about that. I never studied very much.

KING:
They always want more data because they are not sure. Doubt is very inefficient.

KING:
You know what I rely on? Instinct. Common sense. Confidence. Three things they can't measure. That's why they don't like them.

KING:
I have very expensive instincts, by the way. I've spent billions of taxpayer dollars just following my instincts. People tell me they're the biggest instincts anybody's ever seen.

ANNOUNCER:
Your Majesty, the Ministry of Information has prepared this cheerful little musical reminder that so-called experts arguing about silly details inhibit progress.

[♪ NOBODY KNOWS MORE ♪]
