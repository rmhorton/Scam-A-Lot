
“Forgotten Man”

Through-composed heartland country-rock anthem. Continuous vocal delivery, short intro, minimal instrumental breaks, no repeated choruses, no extended outro. Veterans-bar atmosphere that gradually transforms into a modern authoritarian movement anthem. Warm acoustic guitar, Hammond organ, restrained drums, rough male singalong vocals. Under 4 minutes.
