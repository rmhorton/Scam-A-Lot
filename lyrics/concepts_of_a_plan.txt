# CONCEPTS OF A PLAN

Fast satirical musical-theatre patter song over a jaunty political march. Smug male baritone lead with crisp, rapid diction; small mixed chorus only in marked choruses. Brass, piano, woodwinds, snare, and tuba. Straight-faced delivery with sharp comic pauses. Begin singing immediately, maintain continuous momentum, minimal instrumental breaks, no added repetitions, under four minutes, abrupt ending.

[INTRO - FACTIONS ASSEMBLE]

We gathered all the factions
To formulate a plan:
The isolationist, the general,
The preacher and the businessman.

Each knew exactly what they wanted;
No two made the same demand.
So after months of careful work,
We’ve got concepts of a plan!

[CHORUS]

Concepts of a plan!
The finest ever made!
Every promise guaranteed
Till the details are displayed.

It offers everything to everyone—
As only nothing can.
We’ll never spoil the concept
By writing down a plan!

[VERSE 1 — ECONOMY AND GOVERNMENT]

We’ll cut your taxes, clear the debt,
And pay for every need.
We’ll raise your wages, lower costs,
While profits still increase.

We’ll shrink the federal government
And make it twice as strong.
We’ll give the states complete control—
Unless a state is wrong.

[HEALTH CARE]

We promised better coverage
At half of what you pay;
You’ll always keep your doctor,
And the mandates go away.

We’ll cover every illness;
No coverage shall be lost—
And how will we provide all that?
We haven’t priced the cost.

Nobody knew that health care
Could be so complicated!
In two weeks all the details
Will be fully formulated.

[VERSE 2 — TARIFFS, IMMIGRATION, AND TRADE]

We’ll place tariffs on imports;
The foreigners will pay.
Your prices will come tumbling down;
Your factories will stay.

Unless the cost is passed along—
Which no one needs to say.
It raises funds, revives our plants,
And lowers prices every day!

A tariff can do anything
I claim that it will do.
It brings in billions from abroad
And makes things cheaper, too!

We’ll deport the farmworkers
And cut your grocery bill;
The growers will pay higher wages,
But prices tumble still.

We’ll make the dollar powerful
And make our exports soar;
The less we buy from other lands,
The more they’ll buy—for sure!

[WAR AND PEACE]

I am the President of Peace!
Our enemies must fall.
The War Department assures us
We can have no war at all—

Provided they surrender
Before our missiles fly.
We’ll never start another war;
We’ll just end it from the sky.

[BRIDGE — BUDGETS AND TRADEOFFS]

We’ll keep your pension, cut the debt,
Give war and ICE their share;
We’ll pay for it by cutting waste—
We’re certain it’s somewhere.

We took out every mechanism—
It made things complicated.
We took out all the estimates—
They left folks agitated.

We took out every tradeoff
That might disappoint a fan;
And after taking everything out,
We had concepts of a plan!

[FINAL CATALOG]

We’ll make you free by banning things,
Restore the past with something new,
Keep government out of your life
While watching everything you do.

We’ll trust the people absolutely—
Provided they understand:
The vote is only honest
If it goes just as we planned.

[FINAL CHORUS]

Concepts of a plan!
The finest ever made!
Every promise guaranteed
Till the details are displayed.

It offers everything to everyone—
As only nothing can.
We’ll never spoil the concept
By writing down a plan!
