# The Royal Hour with His Royal Highness

## Scene 1 — Price proves beauty.

[Solemn, reverential, state-radio voice over royal fanfare]

ANNOUNCER:
From the Royal Palace in glorious Scam-A-Lot, this is the Ministry of Information.

ANNOUNCER:
Welcome to The Royal Hour, featuring His Most Excellent Majesty, Trumpus Maximus, First King of Scam-A-Lot (Primus Rex Scamalotiae), Founder of the Royal Dynasty, Undefeated in Any Legitimate Election, Paragon of Prosperity, Builder of Greatness, Serial Founder of Hundreds of Enterprises, Bestselling Author, International Golf Champion, Reality Television Actor, Luxury Brand Ambassador, and Self-Proclaimed Stable Genius.

ANNOUNCER:
As always, today's program begins with His Majesty's Personal Royal Anthem.

[**♪ HAIL TO THE THIEF ♪**]

KING [self-absorbed]:

Thank you. Beautiful. Absolutely beautiful. ... Every time I hear that anthem I get a little emotional. People tell me it's the greatest personal royal anthem ever written. Better than all the others. Much catchier. ... Not every country gets a king with his own anthem, you know. Most countries have to share one. ... Sad. ...Kings throughout history had songs written about them. Nice songs. But never like this. Nobody has a better personal anthem.

ANNOUNCER [slightly impatient]:
We have our first caller, Your Majesty.

CALLER1 [anxious]: 
Good morning, Your Highness. My wife and I both have good jobs, but housing prices have gone so high that—

KING [Excited]:

Housing! I know housing better than anybody. I've built thousands of buildings. Beautiful buildings. You know they are beautiful because people paid so much for them. If they weren't beautiful, nobody would pay that much. That's called the market. Did you know I won the Royal Builder of the Year Award? Fifty-three times. More than anybody. More then anybody.

KING:
Before me architecture was very boring. Boxes! Everywhere! Then I came along. Gold. Marble. My name in enormous letters. Suddenly people enjoyed looking at buildings again.


KING:
You know what the trouble with affordable housing is? Nobody ever asks whether it's beautiful housing. That's the problem. When people look at MY buildings, they think about how much somebody paid for them. That's how you know they're beautiful. That's leadership. That's vision.
