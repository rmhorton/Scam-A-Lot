SUPERPOWER SUICIDE

Heavy metal protest anthem. Furious, ominous, riff-driven, with a powerful shouted chorus and clearly intelligible lyrics.

[The title is borrowed from Professor Timothy Snyder https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pe1SxTyhqc]

[INTRO]

We kept waiting for the war,
The final Armageddon
Be careful what you wish for
We didn't hear this comin'.

[VERSE 1]

We never dealt with hatred;
We dressed it up as pride.
Kept the flags of slavery
And rooted for our side.
They pointed out new groups of people
To fear and to despise.
They found the cracks we’d left for them
And worked them open wide.

[PRE-CHORUS]

They learned our fears.
They fed our pride.
They sold the poison.
We subscribed.

[CHORUS]

SUPERPOWER SUICIDE!
Wave the flag and wreck the nation!
SUPERPOWER SUICIDE!
Call surrender liberation!
We tore down everything we built
We did it from inside
They said they'd make us great again.
THEY TALKED US INTO SUICIDE!

[VERSE 2 — fast, relentless]

One more click to feed the rage,
New enemies to blame.
Down the rabbit holes we went;
Different paths, results the same.
The billionaires bought up the news,
The platforms and the feed.
We called them geniuses and bowed,
And let them take the lead.

[PRE-CHORUS]

They sold us fear.
They sold us lies.
They sold our anger
To both sides.

[CHORUS]

SUPERPOWER SUICIDE!

SUPERPOWER SUICIDE!
Call surrender liberation!
We tore down everything we built

THEY TALKED US INTO SUICIDE!

[VERSE 3 — heavier; deliberate]

Fire the ones who know their jobs;
Knowledge gives them power
That just makes them dangerous
Tear down the ivory tower
Fire watchdogs, purge the ranks;
Objections are denied.
The chosen one will lead us
Don't question if he lied.

[Breakdown — half-time, commanding voice]
Faith does not need evidence.
Only heretics need proof.
Do not test or verify
What we believe is truth
And when the promised glory fails?
FIND THE TRAITORS! FILL THE JAILS!

[BRIDGE — ominous, then rising]

They know what subtle flattery
Can make a rich man do:
“Only great men like ourselves
Can see this crisis through.”
A whisper to a billionaire,
A grievance multiplied;
Allies branded enemies,
True enemies welcomed inside.

We were watching for invaders
We filled our army with crusaders
But it was businessmen and traders
Who turned out to be the traitors;
Our nation fell to corporate raiders

Some did it for the money.
Some did it for the throne.
They had us do the work for them;
Institutions overthrown.

SUPERPOWER SUICIDE!
