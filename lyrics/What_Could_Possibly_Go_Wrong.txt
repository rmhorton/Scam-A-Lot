What Could Possibly Go Wrong?

Fast political rally rock song. Bass-baritone male lead, driving drums and guitars, continuous vocals, minimal intro, no long instrumental sections. Confident, triumphant, increasingly overconfident.

Goodhart’s Law set to music. The downside of optimizing for short term savings.

[Verse 1 - Oversight as waste]

We cut a hundred agencies.
We saved a billion bucks.
We don’t need all those PhDs
And bureaucratic schmucks.

They were wasting all that money
On "fraud, waste, and abuse".
We put an end to all that nonsense
And set our business loose!

[Chorus 1]

What could possibly go wrong?
We’ve finally cut the waste.
All those experts everywhere
Were slowing down the pace!

⸻

[Verse 2 - Regulation as abuse]

We slashed the red tape overnight.
We threw their rules away.
Now businesses can do their business
Without stupid delay.

We fired all the watchdogs.
We don’t need all that fuss.
They only kept investigating
Honest businessmen like us.


[Chorus 2]

What could possibly go wrong?
We’ve cut all the red tape
This is what you've wanted for so long
To get the economy in shape

⸻

[Verse 3 - Research as fraud]

If you spend taxpayers' money,
Make something people need.
Everybody knows it's just another paper
Nobody’s going to read.

They say their grants are "peer reviewed"
Believe me, that’s what's wrong.
They only ask the other scientists
Who’ve been in on it all along!

And they’re all just woke professors
Who hate our way of life.
If a study makes us look bad,
Why should we pay the price?


[Chorus 3]

What could possibly go wrong?
They do their own review!
Scientists approving scientists —
That's how they swindle you!

⸻

[Verse 4 - Loyalty over expertise]

I won the election!
That’s all you need to know.
Why should unelected bureaucrats
Ever tell me "No"?

They questioned every project
With studies and appeals
Loyal people don't ask these questions;
They focus on the deals.

Why waste time on bidding?
It only slows us down.
We know exactly who deserves
The contracts in this town.

[Chorus 4]

What could possibly go wrong?
I don't want to hear them whine!
Nobody asks me "Are you sure?"
Because everything is fine!

⸻

[Verse 5 - Prevention is invisible]

We closed another laboratory.
We merged another board.
We cancelled another cancer study.
Efficiency is our reward!

Nobody ever noticed
What those people used to do.
If nobody can see it,
Why send the bill to you?

[Chorus]

What could possibly go wrong?
It can’t be a mistake.
You don’t need earthquake preparation
If there is no earthquake!

⸻

[Verse 6: Review]

We fired all the specialists.
We cut bureaucracy!
We finally made the government
Efficient as can be.

We finally balanced every book!
We simply get things done.
Tomorrow takes care of itself
We have already won!

[Chorus 6]

What could possibly go wrong?
Cut things that can’t be seen!
There’s never been a government
So profitable and lean!

Nobody’s ever seen a nation
So hot and so great!
We cut the waste! We cut the rules!
We've built the perfect state!
