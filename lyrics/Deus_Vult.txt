Deus Vult

Aria for Sir Bombsalot, Minister of Forever War

Fast operatic patter solo for a single powerful bass-baritone over a vigorous Sousa-style American military march. Serious, straight-faced delivery; rapid syllabic singing and speech-like recitative. Minimal instrumental breaks, abrupt ending.

[FAST SECCO RECITATIVE]

Hark! Once again the trumpet calls!
Once more the banners fly!
The ancient foe has a new name,
Yet still his doom draws nigh!

Blessed be the Lord, my rock,
Who trains these hands for war!
The Crusades have never ended!
They’ve reached another shore!

From prophets come our battle prayers,
From Hollywood, some more!
What matter whence the scripture comes,
If it inspires war?

[CHANT]

Deus vult! Deus vult!
Let every warrior exult!

[RECITATIVE — CONTEMPTUOUS]

Yet scribes and lawyers vex us,
And bid the warrior wait:
“Rules of engagement!” “Civilian harm!”
“Proportionality!” “Restraint!”

[SHOUTED]
Fie!

Shall red tape bind the warrior's hands
When righteous battle calls?
Shall armies be commanded
By clerks behind safe walls?

[FAST]

Maximum lethality!
Not tepid legality!
Deus vult! Deus vult!
Let every warrior exult!

[RECITATIVE]

Yet what foul canker still remains?

[spoken with mounting disbelief and disgust]

The "Civilian... Protection... Center...
of Excellence" they say!
But excellence in battle means
To smite without delay!

Zounds!

Each warrior, thirty years or more,
Shall have his blood assayed!
If testosterone be running low,
By God, it shall be raised!

Away with laws and no-strike lists!
Let all our warbirds soar!
We have a High-T War Department —
Let loose the dogs of war!

[FASTER]

And lo! Upon the southern seas
Suspicious vessels crawl!
The King hath named them terrorists —
His word convicteth all!

What ho! Two wretches yet survive
Hellfire's deadly squall!
Upon the shattered wreck they cling —
No quarter!

[SHARP BRASS AND SNARE HIT]

Kill them all!


[DARKER]

At Saada Migrant Detention Center,
Ethiopians were there —
Travelers seeking Saudi jobs,
Till death came through the air.

[SOMBER, RESTRAINED]

Of course civilians suffer most
When mighty armies vie.
Ten dozen tiny scholars dead
Beneath the rubble lie.

A Tomahawk had struck the school.
Minab shall ever speak of grief!
“Iran also has some Tomahawks.”
Said the Commander in Chief.

[SPOKEN ONLY, UNACCOMPANIED]

But they have no Tomahawks...
Forsooth...
Those poor little girls...

[TWO BEATS OF SILENCE]

[MILITARY MARCH RESUMES]

Hark! The drums!

Once more the trumpet calleth!
Once more the banners fly!
Release the iron falcons!
Let dragons fill the sky!

Maximum lethality!
Not tepid legality!
Deus vult! Deus vult!
Let every warrior exult!

The Crusades have never ended!
They've reached another shore!
While wickedness survives on Earth—
THERE SHALL BE RIGHTEOUS WAR!
