# FATHERS AND SONS

Jaunty, comic midtempo bluegrass-country song. Prominent rolling banjo, acoustic guitar, upright bass, fiddle and mandolin, with close male family harmonies on “Fathers and sons.” Wholesome, old-fashioned front-porch warmth; cheerful and faintly self-satisfied, never mournful or cartoonish. Brief banjo-and-fiddle turnarounds after the backing-vocal couplets. Strip down for the bridge, then build into a full final chorus. Minimal intro and short, dry ending.
