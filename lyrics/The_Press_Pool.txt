# THE PRESS POOL

Buoyant late-1970s television-news pop crossed with elegant poolside easy listening. Cool, confident female alto lead with clear, restrained network-anchor delivery; no belting or theatrical acting. Electric piano, clean guitar, melodic bass, crisp drums, vibraphone and bright brass. Breezy female backing harmonies on every “Welcome to the Press Pool” chorus. Glossy, respectable and sincerely upbeat, with a mock-patriotic build near the end. Minimal intro, tight arrangement, sharp brass sting after “Department of War,” short clean ending.

[OPENING REFRAIN]

Welcome to the Press Pool—
Come on in, the water’s fine.
We drained out all the journalists
And filled it up online.

[VERSE 1]

Tim Pool is independent,
A fearless newsman without peer.
He sees through every falsehood
The establishment engineers.

[CHORUS]

Welcome to the Press Pool—
Meet Tim Pool, brave and free.
He never knew who paid him,
So how biased could he be?

[VERSE 2]

A mysterious new patron
Put him on a handsome fee:
A hundred grand per video—
No strings that he could see.

[BRIDGE]

Nobody told him what to say;
Nobody had to steer.
They paid him for his honest thoughts—
Exactly what they hoped to hear.

[VERSE 3]

When the Russian money surfaced,
He said Moscow must have tricked him.
Our fearless investigator cried,
“I’m just another victim!”

[CHORUS]

Welcome to the Press Pool—
Meet Tim Pool, brave and free.
He never knew who paid him,
So how biased could he be?

[VERSE 4]

The inquiry was abandoned;
The founders came back home.
The watchdogs lost their offices;
The assets freely roam.

[VERSE 5]

The seasoned correspondents
Refused the Pentagon’s new rules.
They surrendered their credentials;
The brass welcomed Tim Pool.

[BUILD]

He couldn’t spot the propaganda
Behind a hundred grand—
So they gave him press credentials
At the War Department’s command.

[FINAL CHORUS]

Welcome to the Press Pool—
Tim has joined the chosen few.
Foreign propaganda fooled him,
So his press pass came right through.

Welcome to the Press Pool—
Where gullibility raises your score:
If you can’t detect the Kremlin,
You can cover the Department of War.

[OUTRO]

The Pentagon needs journalists
Who recognize the rule:
Never question friendly sources—
Welcome to the Press Pool.
