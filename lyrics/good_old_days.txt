The Good Old Days

    Nostalgic mid-tempo 1970s country-folk singalong with warm older male lead, acoustic guitar, piano, upright bass, brushed drums, and touches of fiddle and pedal steel. Begin intimate, sincere, and reassuring; gradually build into a cheerful communal anthem as the contradictions become more blatant. The singer remains completely earnest and oblivious—no winking or villainous delivery. Catchy “good old days” refrain, clear diction, conversational phrasing, minimal intro, continuous vocal, and a strong, decisive ending.

[VERSE 1: ELBOW ROOM]

I grew up in a quiet town,
No traffic clogging up the highways,
No crowds along the sidewalks—
Those were the good old days.

The grocer knew us all by name,
The neighbors stopped to talk;
The children played till supper time
On every tree-lined block.

My folks raised six kids in our house,
With cousins down the street;
Before the town got overcrowded,
Life was quiet, life was sweet.

This beach used to be so empty
You could always find a place.
Now umbrellas stretch for miles and miles,
With tourists in your space.

[CHORUS]

Where did all these people come from?
It never used to be this way.
There was room for every family
In the good old days.

[VERSE 2: TRADITIONAL FAMILY VALUES]

People saved themselves for marriage;
Wedding vows were meant for life.
Every boy hoped for a family,
Every girl to be a wife.

Motherhood and apple pie
Were things that we all praised.
Every house was filled with children,
That’s how we were raised.

Life was simple! Life was good!
Girls were girls and men were men!
Why can't we just go back to
The way things were back then?

Everyone should have their freedom—
That’s the good old-fashioned way.
But we all knew what was proper,
And no one went astray.

All these gays and trans and lesbians.
Doing crazy things we never did
Why can't they just be normal
Get married and have kids?

[BRIDGE]

We need less crowded highways,
Fewer houses, fewer cars!
Everyone should just settle down
And raise families like ours.

[CHORUS]

Where did all these people come from?
It never used to be this way.
There was room for normal families
In the good old days.

There was room for normal families
In the good old days.
