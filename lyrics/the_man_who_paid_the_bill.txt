The Man Who Paid the Bill in the Bahamas

Playful ragtime duet, male and female voices alternating lines in verses and joining on repeated final lines. Cxuberant small ensemble choruses jump to a noticeably faster tempo with crisp rapid diction. Snappy Edwardian music-hall instrumentation with syncopated upright piano, banjo, clarinet, muted brass, tuba and crisp snare. Add lively 8-bar ragtime instrumental interludes between each chorus and the following verse, plus a brief instrumental introduction and dance break before the final chorus. Cheerful theatrical delivery with increasingly sinister implications; strong repeated ending.

Exclude:
spoken dialogue, modern pop, rock, synths
