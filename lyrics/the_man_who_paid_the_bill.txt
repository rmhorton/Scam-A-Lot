The Man Who Paid the Bill in the Bahamas

Snappy Edwardian music-hall ragtime with two equal featured soloists, one male and one female. Follow all vocal assignments exactly. In every BOTH LEADS TOGETHER section, the male and female voices must enter simultaneously and sing every word in audible two-part harmony; neither voice may sing alone. In every fast chorus, both featured soloists remain prominent together from beginning to end, supported by a lively ensemble. Never substitute the male soloist for the female lead, never make a duet passage male-only, and never let the ensemble replace either featured singer. Brisk contrasting choruses, crisp comic diction, upright piano, banjo, clarinet, brass, tuba and snare, with lively ragtime interludes.

Exclude:
spoken dialogue, modern pop, rock, synths
