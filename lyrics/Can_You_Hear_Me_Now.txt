Can You Hear Me Now?

Intimate female vocal, sparse accompaniment, minimal instrumental sections, continuous vocal delivery, avoid long intros, outros, and repeated choruses

Late-night Americana ballad. Intimate female vocals, sparse guitar, soft piano, subtle synth pads and radio-static ambience. Conversational delivery, emotional realism, slow build, minimal percussion. Lonely pirate-radio broadcast after midnight. Focus on lyrics and human connection. No long instrumental sections or repeated choruses.
