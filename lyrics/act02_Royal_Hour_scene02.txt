## Scene 2 — Prosecution proves greatness

ANNOUNCER [solemn state-radio voice over royal fanfare]:
We return to The Royal Hour with another loyal subject on the line:

CALLER2: 
Good morning, Your Majesty. I'm worried about all these trumped-up charges being filed against people who criticize —

KING:
Trumped-up charges! First of all, thank you for using my phrase. Wonderful phrase. Tremendous phrase. People say it all the time now. Before me, nobody ever used the expression "trumped-up." Then I came along. Now everybody says it. They actually named an entire legal concept after me. Think about that. That's influence. It's a tremendous honor.

KING:
Some very nasty people tried using trumped-up charges against ME. Didn't work. The people understood. They knew those charges were fake. They knew I was innocent... or they knew it didn't matter. Either way, we won.
 ...
They only investigate important people. Nobody has ever conducted a witch hunt against an unimportant person. Think about it. The investigations proved my greatness. Frankly, they probably got me elected.

KING:
But they weren't really after me. They were after you. I just happened to be standing in the way. Which tells you how important I am.
